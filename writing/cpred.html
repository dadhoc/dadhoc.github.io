Cyberpunk RED - Letter to Matteo

January 2024.

My son.

I hope you're not reading this letter, choom. I'm writing it just in case something happens to me out there, on the job. Deadman's switch hooked to my vitals, ready to shoot you the message within an hour of a brain scan no-show. Feels weird to be writing this while you sleep so sound, just a few inches away... if anything happens to me, I need to know you'll know your history and where to go. Listen good now.

Your Nonno Matteo and Nonna Caterina were Edgerunners. So am I. Nonno noclip, master of stealing shit without being seen. Proper ninja. Served for Biotech in the old country, doing God only knows what for them. Nonna Bluecollar, instead, was the muscle of the duo. You ever seen a five foot six Piedmontese woman mowing down borgs in some alleyway in Japantown, screaming all hell out in laughter in Italian? It's a preem sight, and that's in your blood too. That's where I was born, y'know. Japantown, 2005. Could barely hear my crying over the gunfire. Wish I could say it was some badass, "cut the cord with a combat knife" type of deal, but the job spec came with complimentary exfil on demand. Good job Nonno noclip was a doc or I wouldn't have been here. I carry on his trade, but you carry his name - his real name. Don't you ever forget that, son.

Grew up in and out of the CZ. You think we have it rough out here in Pacifica? At least we get some nights without some vidiot dorphhead getting zeroed just outside our door. Sleeping in the CZ was a privilege. Never had a "childhood home", for years we slept where we could. It was part of the contract - Felix's contract.

You pay attention now, boy: you ever see a two-timing backstabbing corpo _yono_ called Felix Louis, you delta. No ifs, no buts, that piece of shit is trouble. On the surface, he's just another beav working for Militech, but you gotta know better. Even a dorpher like him doesn't start at a corp in the middle of a Corpo War and stay entry level for two decades. Out here where it matters, he's a Mr. Who who's too much of a pussy to get his own hands dirty. More importantly, he's the bastard who killed your grandfolks. Set them up on a bad job a couple years after the Holocaust, right out where SNC is now. Poor gonks fell for the trap, right into the killzone - it was part of the contract, everything looked nova. Felix wasn't even there to watch his _giri_ resolved, but the _yono_ thinks it's cleared, so as far as you're concerned, boy, it's cleared. Don't seek him out, and don't meet his gaze.

You need to know about Tommy, too. That's your uncle. Haven't seen the son of a good mother since they zeroed our folks, and there's a reason for that. I was lost, scared, alone. I looked up to him, and I saw nothing. When the guns went quiet, his eyes glazed over for a second. Didn't even look my way before he ran off to God knows where. Never went after him, don't even know if he's alive. I don't think he knows about you. My advice is to keep it that way - he's your uncle but he's not family. Remember that.

Now, here's the sitch: if you're reading this, I'm dead meat. Go find Impulse in Heywood. He's a merch pusher, goes by 101 sometimes. Says it's from an old game or something. "Just say the words and I can get ya any hardware ya like", or something like that. Don't matter - you go find him and you tell him One-Up flatlined. Ask for the shard. Got a few thou eddies in there for you, and some emergency contacts. Folks I run with on occasion. Eurobucks won't last you long, but you won't starve. You can trust Impulse - if nothing else, because I do. When I reached Pacifica, he was selling iguana sticks on the roadside. Caught me trying to klep one, had me dead to rights. He coulda shot me that day and I wouldn't have blamed the man. Instead he fed me, gave me a roof, a job. Put me through proper med school so I could make a living with MeatWagon doing what I learned from nonno. He's a bit of a weirdo, but he's a good one.

Next, I want you to find Checkmate. Netrunner I sometimes run with. No clue where he's located, but if you ever need him, he'll know. Impulse might be able to get a meet going. We go back, from the CZ. He was actually running for a puppet under 'saka, back then. Found some nasty shit about the corpo - shocker, I know - and had a change of heart. Chipped into nonna's comms one day looking to defect with some intel. Felix sure liked that. Checkmate runs indie now, as an info bro. You need intel found or scrubbed, he's your guy. He won't do it for free, but he'll hook you up with the ol' One-Up discount. For a fella who coulda outpaced Cunningham, that's nothing to scoff at.

From here on out, it's on you, boy. If you're reading this, everything went to shit and whatever plan I coulda traced went with it. So, find my chooms, get some merch, and try not to use it. Head low, chip in, chip out, like I taught you. Love you, boy. Whether you like it or not, I'm with you, always.

-One-Up
