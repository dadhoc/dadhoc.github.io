   Oct. 10th, 1923

   [fragment retrieved from the derelict fishing vessel Madeleine, last seen approximately 250 nautical miles West of the coast of Nova Scotia, Canada]

   I am afraid this will be my final entry. Our vessel has been under assault for three days by creatures I could never begin to understand in a dozen lifetimes - although I fear I will not even live to see the end of this day. I am trapped within my cabin as I write this. The most primal part of me urges me to fight back; to grab a weapon, fashion one out of this quill if I must, and lash out, but I have come to realise this to be a futile endeavour. It would be foolish to claim, or believe, that my crew lay dead still on the deck, as I know now that these devils from the sea, for how clever they seem to be, have no respect for Man, be it in his life or in his death. To them, my fellow men - Abraham Jenkins, Matthew Alan Gordon and Immanuel Smith, lest their names be forever lost to the waves - are not victims of a senseless attack; they are not valued brothers and fathers and sons, they are not worthy of a noble death. No, to these beasts, they are but flesh. Meat to be pried off bones, dragged back to their holes in the sea and fed to their young, if not worse. We are prey, and they, the true wolves of the seas - and as fate would have it, we have wandered straight into their den.

   I hear them now, those terrible sounds I have heard for three days. Even caught in a storm, through the rain, the thunder and the wind, and the echoes of my men’s cries of agony that still ring in my ears, I hear them. I hear their webbed feet, stomping on the outside deck. I hear their claws tearing through the hull, grabbing and piercing and slashing through the metal; the same claws which have slashed and torn through my leg. I hear their teeth, tearing the bodies outside in chunks, and I can almost see their teeth, glistening with their blood. Those damned jaws, may the Devil that put them in the sea take them back, snapping at each other in lustful hunger. The walls of my cabin are strong against gunfire and other weapons of Man, but these creatures are made of some hellish substance stronger even than that. It is only a matter of time before they break me as they’ve broken my mast, tear through me as they have the cloth in my sails, and find my flesh between their razor fangs.

   This journal, should it ever be retrieved, will be found not in my beloved Madeleine - my third love, after my beautiful daughter and this vessel’s namesake, and Jane, the treasure of a woman who gifted my beautiful child to me - but in her rotting carcass, between what’s left of the arms of a man foolish enough to seek out largess from the sea, who has gained from the sea nothing more than death.

   I hold with me a revolver, and it in turn holds the last of five bullets. The other four are lodged in one or two of the dozens of monsters that swarm my vessel. Their last sibling is, alas, reserved for myself. I have fought and I have lost, but I can still ensure my last act on the God our Lord’s Earth to be the ultimate expression of my human will. I have chosen not to give myself to these demons. They may take my body but they will not take my life. In this final act, I can only hope the Lord to forgive my trespass, for I am a God fearing man, but I tremble at the thought of what evil there could exist that would spawn these horrors.

   I leave not a will, as what is left for me to give can not be quantified or shared, but should this journal ever make its way back to my home in Sherbrooke, I ask only that you, reader, tell my wife and child that I have died fearless in the face of peril. Spare them the details of my gruesome end; cast my remains, should any be retrieved, to the sea that hates me so, and claim it lost in the wreck. And to my fellow seamen, say these words in my stead: the waters that give us our sustenance hold many more secrets than we could ever imagine. Be content with the good that the shallow gives us, and seek not the hidden loot of the depths, for all you will find is a Godless horror beyond the darkest dreams of any man.
